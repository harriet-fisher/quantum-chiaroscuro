Draw

Click to add vertices; click the first vertex or press Enter to close. Backspace or right-click removes the last vertex, Esc cancels, U undoes the last shape, S saves. Glass and off-limits get no qubits and are always black.
Shared edges. New vertices snap to existing vertices (ring) and edges (diamond), so a shape can start on, or run along, a neighbour's edge. Hold Alt to place a vertex freely. When a shape closes, vertices that land on a neighbour's edge are added to it so both shapes share the edge. Drag a vertex or an edge to reshape: yellow vertices are shared and everything sharing them moves together, like perspective editing. Esc cancels a drag, U undoes.
